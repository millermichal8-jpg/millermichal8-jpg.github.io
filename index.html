ForexTradingBot C2 Mail Bridge

A privately operated application that helps its owner monitor subscribed Federal Reserve Board email notifications and keep a traceable record of relevant official news.

The planned connection uses the owner's dedicated Gmail mailbox, an existing owner-controlled cloud helper, and the owner's local C2 system. The helper validates message provenance, records normalized news metadata, and transfers qualified notices to the local news dashboard.

Google OAuth access is granted personally by the mailbox owner. Gmail integration is currently undergoing qualification. Operational use follows the owner's approved source and deployment permissions.

This site provides application information and the privacy policy and terms for that private use.

Operator contact: forextradingbot.c2@gmail.com.
